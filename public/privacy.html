Privacy Policy for AI Signals

Last updated: September 4, 2026

AI Signals is a browser extension designed to help authorized users save relevant AI industry information to a shared team workspace.

1. Information We Process

Account information

When you sign in, AI Signals may process your work email address and account identity in order to authenticate you and determine whether you are authorized to access the team workspace.

Authentication credentials, such as access and refresh tokens, may be stored locally in your browser to maintain your signed-in session.

Content you choose to save

When you explicitly use the Capture function, AI Signals may process information from the webpage you are viewing, including:

  • Page title
  • Page URL and website domain
  • Relevant webpage text
  • Image URLs and image metadata
  • Category or classification information
  • Optional comments or thoughts that you choose to enter

This information is used to save the selected content to the AI Signals team workspace.

AI Signals does not sell browsing data and does not use webpage content for advertising.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by AI Signals is used only to:

  • Authenticate authorized users
  • Save user-selected content to the team workspace
  • Organize and analyze saved industry signals
  • Maintain the user's extension and dashboard session
  • Protect the service from unauthorized access

3. Browsing Activity

The extension may operate on webpages in order to provide its Capture interface and extract content when requested by the user.

AI Signals does not intentionally collect or transmit your general browsing history. Page content is transmitted to the AI Signals service when you explicitly choose to capture and save that content.

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

AI Signals uses cloud infrastructure and service providers, including Netlify and Supabase, to operate the application, authenticate users, and store workspace data.

Authentication information may also be stored locally using Chrome extension storage.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data.

Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate AI Signals and the associated team workspace.

Content saved to the team workspace may be accessible to other authorized members of that workspace.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials and stored workspace information. Access to the workspace is limited to authorized users.

7. Data Retention

Saved content may remain in the team workspace until it is removed by an authorized user or administrator.

Locally stored authentication information is cleared when the user signs out or when authorization is revoked.

8. Your Choices

You may:

  • Choose whether to capture and save a webpage
  • Sign out of the extension
  • Disable or uninstall the extension
  • Request removal of saved workspace data through the workspace administrator

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of AI Signals change.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or AI Signals data practices, please contact the AI Signals workspace administrator through your organization's internal support channel.
