# Colleague pilot feedback

Collect this feedback after the first use and again at the end of week two.
Record role and workflow, not personal or article-sensitive information.

## Tasks

For each task, record success, time, and any point where help was needed:

1. Sign in and confirm the correct workspace.
2. Add a new article from the dashboard.
3. Add an existing article and understand the duplicate result.
4. Find another colleague's article.
5. Connect an article to a topic.
6. Understand pending versus AI-reviewed status.
7. Edit an item at the same time as another colleague.
8. Recover from a network interruption.
9. Archive an item; an admin restores a removed item.

## Questions

- What was easier here than in the SharePoint list?
- What was harder or unclear?
- Did you ever wonder whether a change had saved?
- Were contributor, editorial, and pipeline states understandable?
- Did duplicate handling preserve what you expected?
- What information or view from SharePoint was missing?
- Would you trust this as the system of record today? Why or why not?
- What single change would most improve your daily use?

## Stop conditions

Pause the pilot and retain SharePoint as authoritative if any participant sees
unexplained data loss, access outside their role, silent overwrite, a duplicate
that resets reviewed content, or an item that cannot be restored.
